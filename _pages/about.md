Welcome! 

I am a last-year PhD candidate in the [Department of Econometrics and Operations Research](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/about/schools/economics-and-management/organization/departments/eor) at Tilburg University, School of Economics and Management. My supervisors are [Prof. dr. Tobias Klein](https://www.tobiasklein.ws/) and [Dr. Christoph Walsh](https://walshc.github.io/). 

I will be joining [UCLA Anderson School of Management](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/) in January 2027 as a Postdoctoral Scholar, under the supervision of [Elisabeth Honka](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/faculty-and-research/marketing/faculty/honka), Associate Professor of Marketing. 

Research Fields
======
Empirical Industrial Organization, Quantitative Marketing, Consumer Search, Digital Economics.

Research Interests
======
- **Empirical Industrial Organization and Quantitative Marketing**, focusing on how consumers make sequential decisions in the presence of market frictions, such as search, learning, and forgetting. I develop structural models and estimation methods to quantify consumer behavior and evaluate the effects of firm strategies and market interventions.

- **Structural Econometrics**, with an emphasis on estimation methods for multi-stage discrete choice models.

- **Economics of Digital Platforms**.
